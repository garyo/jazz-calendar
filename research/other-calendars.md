# Other Boston music calendars

Candidates for an "Other music calendars" section on the site. Checked 2026-11-07: "live"
means the page answered and showed current listings; "bot-walled" means it answered our
check with 403, so it couldn't be verified, though it works in a browser.

## Jazz

| Calendar | URL | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| JazzBoston Jazz Calendar | https://jazzboston.org/jazz-calendar/ | Live. Built from ArtsBoston's jazz/blues listings. |
| Charlie Kohlhase's Weekly Jazz Calendar Highlights | https://jazzboston.org/charlie-kohlhases-weekly-jazz-calendar-highlights/ | Live. Hand-picked weekly. |
| JazzBoston Jazz Jams | https://jazzboston.org/jazzjams/ | Live. Standing jams. |
| JazzNearYou Boston | https://www.jazznearyou.com/boston/ | Bot-walled. |

## Classical, new music and choral

| Calendar | URL | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Boston Classical Review | https://www.bostonclassicalreview.com/calendar/ | Bot-walled. Pauses in summer. |
| New Music Calendar (Boston) | https://newmusiccalendar.com/boston | Live. Contemporary concert music; listings load by script. |
| Boston Singers' Resource Vocal Music Calendar | https://bostonsingersresource.org/events/ | Live. Choral and vocal; 42 upcoming. |

| Early Music America | https://www.earlymusicamerica.org/events/ | Live. National member calendar; Boston groups appear. |
