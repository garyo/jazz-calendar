        About

        Boston Jazz Calendar is a free, non-commercial, independent listing, run by a local jazz musician and not affiliated with any venue, artist or promoter. No ads, no cookies; we count visits anonymously with our own analytics, which stores no IP addresses or personal data.

        Listings come from venues’ own sites, public calendars and Ticketmaster’s public listings. Our collector, BostonJazzCalendarBot, visits each once a day, follows each site’s robots.txt and terms, and takes only the facts: date, time, who’s playing and price. We never copy descriptions or photos, and every listing links back to its source. Musicians and venues can also submit gigs; we check each one before it appears.

        Plans change: check the venue’s page before you go.

        Listings & corrections

        Playing a gig or running a room? Submit a gig with a link that confirms it; we review every submission before it appears. The form asks for your name and email so we can follow up; they’re never published or shared, and Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you’re not a bot. Spotted a mistake? Write to listings@bostonjazzcalendar.com.

        Venues and artists who’d rather not be listed can write to the same address; we remove listings within a day of a request.

        Calendar feed of every listing: bostonjazzcalendar.com/calendar.ics. Venue names belong to their owners and appear here only to identify them. No ads, no cookies.
