    About

    Boston Jazz Calendar is an independent listing, not affiliated with any venue, artist or promoter. We gather dates, times and prices from venues’ own sites and public calendars, and every listing links back to its source.

    Plans change: check the venue’s page before you go.

    Listings & corrections

    Playing a gig, running a room, or spotted a mistake? Send the date, time, venue and a link to listings@bostonjazzcalendar.com.

    Venues and artists who’d rather not be listed can write to the same address; we remove listings promptly on request.

    Venue names belong to their owners and appear here only to identify them. No ads, no tracking.
