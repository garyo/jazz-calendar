        About

        Boston Jazz Calendar is a free, non-commercial, independent listing, run by a local jazz musician and not affiliated with any venue, artist or promoter. No ads, no cookies; we count visits anonymously with our own analytics, which stores no IP addresses or personal data.

        Listings come from venues’ own sites and public calendars. Our collector, BostonJazzCalendarBot, visits each once a day, follows each site’s robots.txt and terms, and takes only the facts: date, time, who’s playing and price. We never copy descriptions or photos, and every listing links back to its source.

        Plans change: check the venue’s page before you go.

        Listings & corrections

        Playing a gig, running a room, or spotted a mistake? Send the date, time, venue and a link to listings@bostonjazzcalendar.com.

        Venues and artists who’d rather not be listed can write to the same address; we remove listings promptly on request.
